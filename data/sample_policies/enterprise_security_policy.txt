Enterprise Information Security Policy

1. Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the minimum information security requirements for all employees, contractors, and third parties accessing company systems and data.

2. Access Control
2.1 All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) to access corporate systems, including email, VPN, and cloud applications.
2.2 User access rights must follow the principle of least privilege and be reviewed quarterly by system owners.
2.3 Access credentials must not be shared between individuals under any circumstances.

3. Password Policy
3.1 Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and include a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
3.2 Passwords must be changed every 90 days and cannot repeat the last 5 previously used passwords.
3.3 Default vendor passwords must be changed immediately upon system deployment.

4. Data Classification and Handling
4.1 Data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and Restricted.
4.2 Restricted data must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
4.3 Confidential and Restricted data must not be stored on personal devices or unapproved cloud storage services.

5. Incident Response
5.1 Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of discovery.
5.2 The SOC will classify incidents by severity and initiate the incident response playbook within 30 minutes of a Critical classification.
5.3 A post-incident review report must be completed within 5 business days of incident closure.

6. Remote Work Security
6.1 Employees working remotely must connect to corporate resources exclusively via the approved VPN client.
6.2 Company-issued devices must have endpoint detection and response (EDR) software installed and kept up to date.

7. Security Awareness Training
7.1 All employees must complete mandatory security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter.

8. Contact
For security concerns, contact the IT Security Team at it-security@company.com.
