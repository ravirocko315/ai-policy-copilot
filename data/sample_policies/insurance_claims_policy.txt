Group Health Insurance Claims Policy

1. Overview
This document describes the claims process, coverage limits, and exclusions under the company-sponsored Group Health Insurance Plan administered by SecureLife Insurance.

2. Coverage Limits
2.1 The annual maximum coverage per insured employee is $50,000.
2.2 In-patient hospitalization is covered up to 100% of eligible expenses, subject to the annual maximum.
2.3 Outpatient consultations are reimbursed at 80%, up to $2,000 per year.
2.4 Dental treatment is covered up to $1,000 per year, excluding cosmetic procedures.
2.5 Maternity benefits are capped at $5,000 per pregnancy and require 10 months of continuous enrollment prior to claim eligibility.

3. Claim Submission Process
3.1 Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the date of treatment using the online claims portal or the physical claim form.
3.2 Required documents include the original invoice, a detailed medical report, and the claim form signed by the treating physician.
3.3 Claims are typically processed within 15 business days of complete documentation being received.
3.4 Cashless claims at network hospitals require a pre-authorization form submitted at least 48 hours before planned admission (24 hours for emergencies).

4. Exclusions
4.1 Pre-existing conditions are not covered during the first 12 months of enrollment.
4.2 Cosmetic surgery, fertility treatments, and self-inflicted injuries are excluded from coverage.
4.3 Treatment received outside the approved network without prior authorization may be reimbursed at a reduced rate of 50%.

5. Claim Appeals
5.1 Employees may appeal a rejected claim within 30 days of the rejection notice by submitting an appeal form to the Insurance Grievance Committee.
5.2 Appeals are reviewed and a decision is communicated within 20 business days.

6. Contact
For claims assistance, contact the Insurance Desk at claims@securelife-support.com or call 1-800-555-0199.
