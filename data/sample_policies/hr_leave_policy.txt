HR Leave and Time-Off Policy

1. Purpose
This policy defines the leave entitlements available to all full-time employees of the company, including annual leave, sick leave, parental leave, and bereavement leave.

2. Annual Leave Entitlement
2.1 Employees are entitled to 21 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 1.75 days per month.
2.2 Unused annual leave may be carried forward to the next calendar year up to a maximum of 5 days.
2.3 Annual leave requests must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance through the HR portal.

3. Sick Leave
3.1 Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year.
3.2 A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive working days.

4. Parental Leave
4.1 Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave.
4.2 Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave.

5. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, contact the HR Help Desk at hr-helpdesk@company.com.
